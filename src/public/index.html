About Me

Hi, nice to meet you! I'm a full time student from Portugal who loves to tinker and explore everything around technology.

In my free time, I enjoy testing, using, and contributing to open-source tools and technologies, developing applications and scripts, planning and managing computer systems (like my home server), and learning new things along the way.

Some of my skills include Linux, Agile Methodologies (Git), containerization (Docker, Podman and Docker compose), system administration and security, networking, programming (mostly Python, C#, Java and C), and system planning/design.

I'm an autodidact who likes to approach things in a problem-solving way. I believe that every thing you do can expand your knowledge while helping you better understand the world around you.

I'm also a big fan of Arch Linux and KDE Plasma.

Currently pursuing a Bachelor's Degree in Computer Sciences Engineering at University of Évora.
